## UPI - Unified Payments Interface

UPI is an instant real-time payment system developed by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) to facilitate inter-bank transactions through mobile phones. It is built over the IMPS infrastructure and allows you to send and receive money instantly using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA).

## How UPI Works

UPI uses a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) or UPI ID as an identifier. A VPA looks like 'username@bankname'. You do not need to remember the beneficiary's account number, IFSC code, or other bank details — just the VPA.

UPI supports:
- Person-to-Person (P2P) transfers
- Person-to-Merchant (P2M) payments
- Bill payments
- QR code-based payments
- Recurring payments and mandates

## Key Details

- Available 24x7, 365 days a year
- Single tap payment for merchants
- No need to share bank account details
- Multiple bank accounts can be linked to a single UPI ID
- Transaction limit per UPI transaction is Rs. 1,00,000 (as per current RBI guidelines)

## HDFC Bank UPI

HDFC Bank UPI services are accessible through:
- HDFC Bank MobileBanking App
- HDFC Bank PayZapp
- Any third-party UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.)
- NetBanking

## HDFC UPI Features

- Instant money transfer to any bank account
- Bill split feature for shared expenses
- QR code scanner for merchant payments
- UPI AutoPay for recurring mandates
- Request money feature

## Eligibility

- All HDFC Bank savings and current account holders
- Must have an active debit card linked to the account for UPI registration
- Mobile number must be registered with the bank
