## RTGS - Real Time Gross Settlement

RTGS is a funds transfer system where money is transferred in real-time and on a gross basis. "Real Time" means the processing of instructions at the moment they are received rather than at some later time. "Gross Settlement" means the settlement of funds transfer instructions occurs individually, not on a net or batch basis.

## How RTGS Works

RTGS is primarily meant for large-value, time-critical transactions. The minimum amount to be remitted through RTGS is Rs. 2,00,000 (two lakh rupees). There is no upper ceiling for RTGS transactions.

## Key Details

- RTGS is available 24x7, 365 days a year
- Minimum transfer amount: Rs. 2,00,000
- No maximum limit for transfers
- Settlement is done individually for each transaction

## HDFC Bank RTGS

HDFC Bank provides RTGS facilities to its customers through:
- All HDFC Bank branches
- HDFC Bank NetBanking
- HDFC Bank MobileBanking App

## Fees and Charges

- As per RBI guidelines and the bank's schedule of charges
- RTGS charges are generally higher than NEFT due to the real-time nature of the transfer

## Eligibility

- All HDFC Bank savings and current account holders
- Must have an active HDFC Bank account with sufficient balance
- IFSC code of the beneficiary bank branch is required
