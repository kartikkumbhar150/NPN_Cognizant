## NEFT - National Electronic Funds Transfer

NEFT is a nation-wide payment system facilitating one-to-one funds transfer. Operated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), NEFT enables individuals, companies, and firms to electronically transfer funds from any bank branch to any individual, company, or firm having an account with any other bank branch in the country participating in the NEFT scheme.

## How NEFT Works

NEFT operates on a deferred settlement basis. Transactions are settled in hourly batches. The settlement batches are available from 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM on all working days of RBI (Monday to Saturday, excluding 2nd and 4th Saturday and all public holidays).

NEFT rounds operate in half-hourly batches from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM and hourly batches from 1:00 PM to 6:30 PM.

## Key Details

- NEFT is available 24x7, 365 days a year
- The minimum transfer amount is Rs. 1
- There is no maximum limit for NEFT transfers
- Transactions initiated after the cut-off time are processed on the next working day

## HDFC Bank NEFT

HDFC Bank offers NEFT facilities through all its branches. Customers can also initiate NEFT transfers through:
- HDFC Bank NetBanking
- HDFC Bank MobileBanking App
- HDFC Bank ATM

## Fees and Charges

- Inward NEFT transactions: No charges
- Outward NEFT transactions: As per RBI guidelines and bank's schedule of charges

## Eligibility

- All HDFC Bank savings and current account holders can use NEFT
- No minimum balance requirement for NEFT activation
