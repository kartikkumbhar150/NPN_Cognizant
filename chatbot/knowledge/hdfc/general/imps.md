## IMPS - Immediate Payment Service

IMPS is an instant payment inter-bank electronic fund transfer service available 24x7, 365 days a year across all banks in India. IMPS offers an instant, 24x7, interbank electronic fund transfer service through mobile phones.

## How IMPS Works

IMPS is a convenient channel for sending and receiving money using mobile phones. It works through:
- Mobile Number and MMID (Mobile Money Identifier)
- Account Number and IFSC Code
- Aadhaar Number

## Key Details

- Available 24x7, 365 days a year including holidays
- No minimum or maximum transfer limit (subject to bank-specific limits)
- Real-time confirmation of successful transactions
- Instant credit to the beneficiary account

## HDFC Bank IMPS

HDFC Bank offers IMPS through:
- HDFC Bank NetBanking
- HDFC Bank MobileBanking App
- HDFC Bank ATMs
- All HDFC Bank branches

## Fees and Charges

- Charges as per RBI guidelines and HDFC Bank schedule of charges
- Generally nominal compared to RTGS
- Inward IMPS is typically free of charge

## Eligibility

- All HDFC Bank account holders
- Must be registered for mobile banking or internet banking
- MMID registration required for mobile-based IMPS
